YouTube display name "History Recapped".

**Why:** User pivoted on 2026-05-03 from a Hindi Mahabharat concept → war history with sports's footage-cut-in pipeline → 100% archival footage. Sports-style hybrid (illustrated plates + 1 cut at climax) was rejected as "no suspense format in this type of shorts". The right format is pure archival timeline (5-7 footage windows concat'd) with our Cartesia narrator + word captions + inline national-flag emojis. See feedback_history_recapped_format.md for the rule.
